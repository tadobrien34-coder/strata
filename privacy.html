Privacy Policy

Effective 19 September 2026 · Applies to the Strata web game at this address and any home-screen installation of it.

The short version

Strata does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no third-party code. Your progress and settings are saved on your own device and never leave it.

Who is responsible

Strata is operated by [your legal name or business name], [city, state/country]. Contact: [contact email].

What is stored on your device

The game uses your browser's local storage to remember the following between sessions. This is functional storage that the game needs to work as you'd expect; it is not used for tracking and contains nothing that identifies you.

  • Best depth, today's daily-shaft best and the day it was set
  • Coin balance, which skins you own and which one is equipped
  • Sound, music, mute and haptics settings

A service worker also caches the game's files so it can run offline. Nothing in the cache is personal.

What we receive

Nothing. The game makes no network requests after it has loaded, and it has no server of its own.

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you load it, GitHub's servers receive your IP address and standard request details, as any web host does. That is governed by GitHub's privacy statement; we do not have access to those logs.

Sharing your score

The Share button uses your device's own share sheet, or copies text to your clipboard. Nothing is sent to us. What you do with the text after that is up to you.

Cookies

Strata sets no cookies. Because the local storage described above is strictly necessary for the game to function and is not used for tracking or advertising, no consent banner is shown.

Children

Strata is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone, of any age. Because no data is collected, no parental consent mechanism is required. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first and the change will be visible in the game.

Deleting your data

Everything the game stores is on your device, so deletion is in your hands and takes effect immediately:

  • In the game, open the start screen and use Reset all data. This erases every value listed above.
  • Or clear site data for this address in your browser's settings.
  • If you installed the game to your home screen, uninstalling it removes everything.

There is nothing on our side to delete, so there is no request to make. If you have a question anyway, use the contact above.

Your rights

Under laws such as the GDPR and CCPA you have rights to access, correct, delete and port personal data, and to opt out of its sale. Strata holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing to exercise those rights against, but the contact above will answer any question about this within 30 days.

Changes

If the game ever adds advertising, analytics, accounts or purchases, this policy will be rewritten before that happens and the effective date above will change.
